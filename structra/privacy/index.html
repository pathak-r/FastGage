Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This policy describes how FastGage ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Structra app.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address, used to create your account and send one-time passcodes (OTPs) for sign-in.
  • Photos and report content. Photos you capture or upload, and the descriptions, notes, and reports generated from them.
  • Usage and device data. Basic diagnostic information such as app version, device type, and crash logs, used to keep the app working reliably.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: analysing your photos and generating snagging reports.
  • To authenticate you via email OTP.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI processing

Structra uses Google's Gemini API to analyse photos and generate report text. Photos and related prompts you submit are sent to Google for processing, subject to Google's API terms. We do not use your content to train AI models.

Data storage and retention

Your reports and photos are stored securely and retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual reports at any time, and deleting your account removes your data from our systems.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Children

Structra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact
