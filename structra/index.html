How it works

Three steps

01

Set up the unit

Name the inspection. Choose apartment, villa or office. Set how many of each room. Structra names them for you — Bedroom 1, Bathroom 1.

1. Rooms
02

Take the photos

Each room has a seven-shot list: overview, ceiling, walls, floor, windows and doors, fittings, then close-ups of defects. Shoot in the app or pick from your library. Dark or small photos get flagged before you leave.

2. Photos
03

Get the PDF

Check the shots, then generate. You get a summary, severity counts, and each finding with a box on the photo and what to fix. Export the PDF and send it.
