What you get

A report you can send

The walk, the grades, and the photos, in one PDF.

  • A guided walk

    Room by room. Structra names them for you — Bedroom 1, Bathroom 1.

  • Each finding graded

    Critical, Major, Minor, or Observation. You see the counts before you send.

  • A box on the photo

    The crack, stain or gap is marked, so the contractor can see what you mean.

  • A PDF to send

    Summary, severity counts, and what to fix on each finding.

How it works

Three steps

01

Set up the unit

Name the inspection. Choose apartment, villa or office. Set how many of each room. Structra names them for you — Bedroom 1, Bathroom 1.

1. Rooms
02

Take the photos

Each room has a shot list: overview, ceiling, walls, floor, windows and doors, fittings, then close-ups of defects. Shoot in the app or pick from your library.

2. Photos
03

Get the PDF

Check the shots, then generate. You get a summary, severity counts, and each finding with a box on the photo and what to fix. Export the PDF and send it.
